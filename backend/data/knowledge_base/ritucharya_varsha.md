# Varsha Ritu (Monsoon) Lifestyle and Regimen

During Varsha Ritu (Monsoon), Agni (digestive fire) weakens due to dampness and humidity. Vata dosha accumulates and is prone to aggravation.

Keep warm and dry, consume boiled water infused with ginger or tulsi, avoid sleeping during daytime, and avoid heavy raw salads. Practice gentle oil massage (Abhyanga) with warm sesame oil to balance Vata.
