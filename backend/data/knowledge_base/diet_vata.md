# Vata Pacifying Diet Guidelines

Warm, cooked, unctuous, and nourishing meals are essential for balancing Vata dosha. Cold, dry, raw foods aggravate Vata leading to dryness, constipation, and restlessness.

Favor sweet, sour, and salty tastes. Use warming spices such as ginger, cumin, cinnamon, and cardamom. Healthy fats like cow's ghee, sesame oil, and warm milk ground and pacify airy Vata nature.
