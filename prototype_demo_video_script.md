## 💡 Quick Tips for Recording:
1. **Screen Resolution:** Record in 1080p (1920x1080) at 60 FPS (using OBS Studio or Windows Game Bar `Win + G`).
2. **Audio:** Use a clean collar mic or earphones. Speak with warmth, steady pace, and conviction.
3. **Mouse Movement:** Keep cursor movements smooth and intentional — don't make jittery circles on screen.
4. **Bilingual Switch:** Toggling between English and Hindi during Scene 5 creates an instant *"WOW"* factor for government hackathon evaluators!
